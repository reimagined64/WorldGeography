COUNTRY REFERENCE FACTS
CountryInfo 0.1.2 supplied initial country capitals and representative positions.
Many entries were corrected and Czech labels added. Its historical population
figures are NOT used. CountryInfo describes its reference sources as Wikipedia,
REST Countries, and GeoNames. ISO codes were matched with pycountry 24.6.1;
pycountry implementation is not redistributed. See data/sources.json.
